# Opportunity-Tracking-Direct-vs.-Partner-Channel-Performance
This dashboard analyzes Direct and Partner sales channels to uncover revenue trends, deal performance, regional opportunities, product and customer insights, and sales funnel patterns. It highlights key growth opportunities and pipeline gaps to support stronger sales strategies, improve conversion, and enable data-driven business decisions.

## KEY INSIGHTS

* The **Partner Channel is the leading revenue contributor**, generating approximately **$295M (63.95%)** of total revenue, while the Direct Channel contributes **$166M (36.05%)**.

* Partner opportunities have a significantly higher **average deal value of $5.4M**, compared with **$3.0M** for Direct opportunities.

* High-value opportunities are concentrated within the Partner Channel, with **138 large deals** compared with only **24 large deals** through the Direct Channel.

* The **East Region** is the strongest-performing market, recording the highest overall revenue and opportunity activity. The **West Region** has the lowest performance, presenting a clear opportunity for growth.

* Revenue demonstrates a noticeable **seasonal pattern**, with a major peak early in the year, followed by periods of decline and smaller recoveries toward the end of the year.

* The sales pipeline experiences substantial **opportunity attrition across different stages**, from Lead and Qualification through Solution, Proposal, and Finalization. This indicates potential gaps in the conversion process.

* **Sontaxon** is the highest-performing product, generating approximately **$48M in revenue**, while several other products contribute significantly less.

* **Strategic and Large customers** account for the highest revenue contributions, highlighting the importance of high-value customer segments to overall business performance.

* The Direct Channel has significant potential to increase its contribution by improving its ability to secure larger opportunities and increase average deal value.

## RECOMMENDATIONS

* Expand and strengthen the **Partner Channel** through strategic partnerships, incentive programmes, and partner enablement, given its dominant contribution to revenue.

* Improve **Direct Channel performance** by strengthening enterprise sales capabilities, negotiation strategies, account management, and deal-expansion techniques.

* Develop targeted acquisition and retention strategies for **Strategic and Large customers** to protect and grow high-value revenue streams.

* Analyze pipeline drop-offs to identify the specific stages where opportunities are being lost and introduce measures to improve **lead qualification, engagement, proposal development, and conversion**.

* Increase investment in the **West Region** through targeted marketing, stronger sales coverage, partnerships, and customer acquisition initiatives.

* Reduce product concentration by promoting **cross-selling, upselling, and product bundling** for lower-performing products.

* Strengthen relationships with Strategic customers through dedicated account management and proactive retention programmes.

* Use **seasonal sales campaigns and targeted promotions** during weaker periods to create more consistent revenue throughout the year.

* Monitor the sales cycle across Direct and Partner channels to identify bottlenecks and improve operational efficiency through process optimization and automation.

* Identify successful strategies from the **East Region** and adapt them across Central and West markets to improve regional performance.

* Track key performance indicators regularly, including **revenue by channel, average deal size, conversion rate, sales cycle duration, regional revenue, product performance, and customer segment contribution**.
